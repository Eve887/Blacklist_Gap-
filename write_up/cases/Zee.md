# Case file: Zee Laboratories Ltd

Part of "The Blacklist Gap" review. Buyers may have reasons not visible in
public records; the firm was not contacted for comment. Every finding
below is a possible breach of a tender clause as worded, not a finding of
fraud or illegal conduct.

## Headline finding

Madhya Pradesh's MPPHSCL banned 3 of Zee's injectable products as "Not of
Standard Quality" in 2024, each confirmed by the Central Drugs Laboratory
(CDL), Kolkata - a statutory testing authority (Q1, in this review's own
terms - see `Blacklist_Gap_Report.md` section 3). Zee separately holds 73
rate-contract lines with Rajasthan's RMSC across the 3 drug tenders where
all 3 of these bans were active on the bid deadline. Under RMSC's own
clause 5(l)(II), read as worded, this is a **possible breach of the
tender clause as worded**.

## Policy gap: the CMC eye-drops case

Because PMBI's debarment (18-12-2024) began **after** Rajasthan's own
NIB-10/2024 bid deadline (08-10-2024), it does not make Zee ineligible to
bid under clause 5(l)(II) - the gap only opens once the Rajasthan contract
for the exact same product is already running (from 31-05-2025). See
`Blacklist_Gap_Report.md` section 4(b) for the full discussion of why
RMSC's own mid-contract cancellation clause (General Conditions, item (o),
page 25) would not reach this specific case either.
