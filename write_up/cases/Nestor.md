# Case file: Nestor Pharmaceuticals Ltd

Part of "The Blacklist Gap" review. Buyers may have reasons not visible in
public records; the firm was not contacted for comment. Every finding
below is a possible breach of a tender clause as worded, not a finding of
fraud or illegal conduct.

## Headline finding

Andhra Pradesh's APMSIDC banned Nestor's Iron & Folic Acid Syrup IP
(20mg+0.1mg) for 2 years after the Drugs Control Laboratory (DCL),
Vijayawada - a statutory lab (Q1) - found it "Not of Standard Quality"
across 4 batches. Nestor separately holds 4 rate-contract lines with
Rajasthan's RMSC under the drug tender NIB-14/2024, active on that
tender's own bid deadline. Under RMSC's clause 5(l)(II), this is a
**possible breach of the tender clause as worded**.

## Outside evidence: the CAG Haryana finding (a separate buyer pair)

A Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India report, "Public Health
Infrastructure and Management of Health Services", tabled in the Haryana
Vidhan Sabha and reported by The Tribune (14-11-2024), found that Haryana
Medical Services Corporation Ltd (HMSCL) purchased drugs worth an
estimated Rs 1.52 crore from Nestor Pharmaceuticals between May and
December 2019 - for the same drug (folic acid-ferrous sulphate tablets) -
**after** Gujarat's GMSCL had already blacklisted Nestor for 3 years in
February 2017 for supplying that exact drug, and after Haryana itself
separately blacklisted Nestor in September 2018 for concealing that
earlier Gujarat action. This is a DIFFERENT buyer pair (Gujarat/Haryana,
not Gujarat/Rajasthan) from this case file's own headline finding above -
found via a general web search, not supplied with this project's own
source material, and independently confirmed by the CAG's own audit.
https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/haryana-bought-drugs-from-blacklisted-firm-cag/

Note: an earlier draft of this project's own RMSC analysis explored a
possible match between Nestor's APMSIDC ban and a Group B (same-product)
Rajasthan finding; that specific line of analysis was retracted on dosage-
form grounds (syrup banned vs tablets contracted) and does not form part
of this case file's headline finding - only the APMSIDC/RMSC Q1 finding
above does.
