# Resume lines - The Blacklist Gap

3 versions, each covering the regulatory, finance, and data-science sides
of the project.

## 1 line

Reviewed 804 public blacklist records across 9 Indian government drug
buyers, built a rate-contract cross-check and a firm-matching pipeline in
Python, and found 3 cases where a statutory quality ban by one buyer
coincided with an active, unreduced supply contract worth a combined
Rs 29-54 crore (2-year estimated value, not amount paid) with another -
reported with full source traceability and an independent fact-check.

## 2 lines

Independently reviewed public blacklist and rate-contract records from 9
Indian government drug and medical-supply buyers (804 ban records, 2,084
rate-contract lines), reading the relevant tender clause text directly and
building a reproducible Python pipeline for name-matching, quality-finding
classification (Q1/Q2), and cross-buyer date-overlap checks. Identified 3
cases of a statutory quality ban at one buyer coinciding with an active
Rajasthan rate contract worth a combined Rs 29-54 crore (2-year estimated
value, not amount paid) at another, built a firm-buyer network analysis
and a correlational test against a second, independent national dataset,
and had the full report, case files, and every cited figure independently
fact-checked claim by claim.

## 3 bullet points

- **Regulatory analysis**: read and applied the exact eligibility-clause
  wording of a state government tender (Rajasthan's RMSC) to 804 public
  blacklist records from 9 Indian government drug and medical-supply
  buyers, identifying 3 cases of a possible breach of the clause as
  worded and 2 further structural gaps in how the clause, bid-time
  affidavit, and mid-contract cancellation rule relate to each other.
- **Financial estimation**: built a 2-year estimated-contract-value model
  (tender quantity x contracted rate, with an upper and an equal-share
  estimate per shared drug code) across 92 rate-contract lines and 4
  tenders, including hand-reading 2 scanned tenders' quantity tables where
  automated OCR could not reliably keep the source table's columns
  aligned.
- **Data science**: built a reproducible Python pipeline (name
  standardisation and fuzzy matching, a firm<->buyer network chart, cross-
  tabulated summary statistics, and a before/after correlational test
  against a second, independent 6,835-row national dataset), and
  commissioned an independent fact-check of every number, date, and claim
  in the final report before publishing it.
